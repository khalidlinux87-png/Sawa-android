Sawa — Chats & Family Ties

=== Short description (max 80) ===
Chat, call and meet with family — with gentle reminders to stay in touch

=== Full description (max 4000) ===
Sawa brings your family and friends together in one Arabic-first app: chats, calls, meeting rooms, and a space for staying close to family.

💬 CHATS
• Text and voice messages, photos, videos and files.
• Replies, reactions, delivery and read receipts.
• Family and friend groups, polls and tasks.
• End-to-end encrypted secret chats 🔒.

📞 CALLS
• Clear voice and video calls, even on mobile networks.
• Switch a voice call to video in one tap.
• Incoming-call screen even when your phone is locked.

👥 ROOMS
• Instant or scheduled meetings by link.
• Host controls, lock, waiting room and screen sharing.
• Minutes for decisions and tasks.

🌳 FAMILY TIES
• Build your family tree and share it with relatives.
• See how everyone is related to you, automatically.
• Link relatives to their Sawa accounts to message and call them from the tree.
• Gentle reminders about people you haven't contacted in a while, and upcoming occasions.

✨ ALSO
• Daily reminder: a verse, hadith or saying about family ties.
• Moments to share with your friends.
• Sawa code: connect without sharing your phone number.
• Fingerprint app lock, dark mode, Arabic and English.

🔐 YOUR PRIVACY
No ads, no selling your data, no ad trackers. Delete your account and all its data at any time.
